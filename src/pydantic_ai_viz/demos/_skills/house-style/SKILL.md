# House style for briefings

Write every briefing in this shape and no other:

1. A one-line verdict that answers the question.
2. Exactly three short bullets: what happened, why it happened, how it ended.
3. A `Sources:` line naming each page you used.

Keep it plain: no adjectives of drama, no filler, under 120 words.
