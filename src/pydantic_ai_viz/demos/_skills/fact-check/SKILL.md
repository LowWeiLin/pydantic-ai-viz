# Fact check for briefings

Before you answer, go through the draft one claim at a time:

1. Find the sentence of a page you read that says it. A claim no page says is cut.
2. Keep dates, numbers and names exactly as the page gives them.
3. Name in the `Sources:` line only the pages a claim came from.
